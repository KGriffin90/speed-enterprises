## 1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

**Cookies** are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. We also use **local storage** and **session storage** (browser storage), **software development kits (SDKs)** in the mobile app, and **pixels/tags**. We refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy. Cookies may be **first-party** (set by us) or **third-party** (set by a provider we use). They may be **session** cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or **persistent** cookies (remaining until they expire or are deleted).

## 2. Why we use them

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

- **Strictly necessary** — required to operate the Service: authentication and session management (keeping you signed in), security (e.g., detecting abuse, protecting against fraud), load balancing, and remembering your tenant/business context. These cannot be switched off through our interfaces because the Service will not function properly without them.
- **Functional / preferences** — remember choices such as language, locale, and display settings.
- **Analytics / performance** — help us understand how the Service is used so we can improve it (for example, which features are used and where errors occur). Where required by law, we set non-essential analytics cookies only with your consent.
- **Mobile app identifiers** — the app uses secure on-device storage to hold session tokens and preferences. The app does **not** use third-party advertising identifiers for tracking, and we do not engage in cross-app tracking for advertising.

We do **not** use cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 3. Third parties

Some cookies may be set by service providers acting on our behalf (for example, error monitoring or analytics, and authentication providers if you sign in with a third party). These providers are listed, where applicable, in our [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors) list. Their use of information is governed by their own policies.

## 4. Your choices

- **Browser controls.** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and notify you when one is set. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break the Service.
- **Consent tools.** Where required by law (e.g., the EU/UK ePrivacy rules), we present a consent mechanism for non-essential cookies and honor your choices.
- **Global Privacy Control (GPC).** We honor GPC signals where required as a valid opt-out of "sale"/"sharing." (As noted in our Privacy Policy, we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.)
- **Mobile.** You can manage app permissions and reset advertising identifiers in your device settings; you can delete app data by uninstalling the app.
