- **When we act as a "controller" / "business."** For information about how the Service itself works — account identity (email, password), platform-level security logs, billing with our business customers, and our own website analytics — {{COMPANY_SHORT_NAME}} decides why and how the data is processed. This policy describes our practices in both roles. Where a Business Customer's own privacy notice conflicts with this policy as to data they control, their notice governs their processing.

## 3. Information we collect

We collect the categories of personal information below. Not every category applies to every person; what we collect depends on how you interact with the Service.

### 3.1 Information you provide directly

- **Account and identity data:** email address, password (stored only as a salted hash — we never store your plaintext password), and, where you choose it, third-party sign-in identifiers (e.g., a Google account identifier if you sign in with Google).
- **Contact and profile data:** full name, phone number, and postal/service address (street, city, state, ZIP/postal code).
- **Quote and job data:** the service requested, answers to job questions, free-text notes you write, preferred appointment times, property type, and similar details you submit.
- **Photos and media:** images you upload of the property, windows, equipment, or job site.
- **Measurements:** dimensions captured via the in-app camera/AR measuring tool (for example, window sizes and counts) and related estimates.
- **Communications:** messages, support requests, and feedback you send us or a Business Customer through the Service.
- **Marketing preferences:** whether you have opted in to marketing communications.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device and connection data:** IP address, browser type, device type and operating system, and user-agent string.
- **Authentication/session data:** session and refresh tokens, login timestamps, and (for security) the IP address and user-agent associated with a session.
- **Usage data:** pages or screens viewed, features used, and interaction timestamps.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy).

### 3.3 Precise location (field/mobile staff only)

If you are field staff for a Business Customer and use the mobile app to track work routes, the app collects **precise geolocation** (latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed, heading, and altitude) **only while you are actively on the clock / running a route with the app in the foreground.** The app does **not** track your location in the background or when you are off the clock. Location is used to record mileage and routes for your employer's operational and payroll purposes. You can disable location permission in your device settings, though doing so will limit route features. We do not collect precise location from consumers/job requesters.

### 3.4 Employment-related data (Business Customer staff only)

For staff of a Business Customer, we may process pay rate/wage information, time entries (clock-in/clock-out, breaks), vehicle/mileage information, and role/permission data, on behalf of and at the direction of the employing Business Customer.

### 3.5 Information from third parties

- **Authentication providers** (e.g., Google) — if you choose to sign in with a third-party account, we receive a unique identifier and basic profile information per that provider's permissions.
- **Business Customers** — a business may create or import a record about you (for example, if you requested service by phone) and enter it into the Service.
- **Service providers** — our infrastructure, email-delivery, storage, and (if applicable) payment providers may provide us delivery, security, and transaction metadata. See [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors).

### 3.6 Sensitive information

We do **not** intentionally collect special categories of data (GDPR Art. 9) or "sensitive personal information" (CPRA) such as government IDs, precise health data, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, or biometric identifiers. **Please do not upload such information.** Precise geolocation of staff is treated as sensitive personal information under some U.S. laws and is handled as described in Section 3.3 — used only for the operational purpose stated and never sold or used for cross-context behavioral advertising.

### 3.7 Children

The Service is intended for users **{{AGE_MINIMUM}} years of age or older** and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age required by applicable law in your jurisdiction, e.g., 16 in parts of the EEA). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and we will delete it.

## 4. How we use information, and our legal bases

We use personal information for the purposes below. For users in the EEA, UK, and other jurisdictions that require a legal basis, the applicable GDPR/UK-GDPR legal basis is noted in brackets.

- **Provide and operate the Service** — create and authenticate your account, route quote requests to the right business, schedule appointments, and deliver core features. *[Performance of a contract; legitimate interests.]*

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we balance those interests against your rights and freedoms; you may object as described in Section 9.

We do **not** use your personal information to make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing without a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards. AI-assisted estimate suggestions are tools for the Business Customer, who remains responsible for any quote or decision.

## 5. How we share information

We disclose personal information only as described here. **We do not sell your personal information for money.**

- **With service providers / processors.** We use vendors for cloud hosting, database hosting, file/object storage, email delivery, error monitoring, and (if enabled) payments. They process data only on our instructions under contract. See [Subprocessors](/legal/subprocessors).
- **For legal reasons.** To comply with law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request; to enforce our terms; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of {{COMPANY_SHORT_NAME}}, our users, or others.
- **Business transfers.** In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to this policy or a successor's policy with comparable protections.
- **With your direction or consent.** When you ask us to share information or otherwise consent.
- **Aggregated or de-identified data.** We may share data that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.
